I am a M.Sc. student at [Politecnico di Milano (Polimi)](https://www.polimi.it/en/) advised by [Professor Krishna P. Gummadi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Bz3APTsAAAAJ&hl=en). My research is focused on Mechanistic Interpretibility of Large Language Models (LLMs), More specifically, I work on knowledge represenation. At the same time, I am also doing my Master in Computer Science at Saarland University. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, under supervision of [Professor Shohreh Kasaei](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mvx4PvgAAAAJ&hl=en). My thesis was focused on enhancing 3D Brain Tumor segmentation using a transformer-based network.
